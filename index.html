Apple · Launch concept

Vision Air

Room to think. Wherever you are. A launch plan for a lighter, lower-priced spatial computer, built for the students and creators who need more screen in less space.

  • $1,499 target price
  • Under 400 g
  • Students and creators under 25
  • Back to School launch

01 · Market research

The best spatial computer, and almost nobody owns one.

Vision Pro launched at $3,499. A faster chip didn’t move demand. The barriers are price, comfort and too few reasons to wear it every day.

Reasons people pass

02 · Target personas

Built for people who are short on space, not on ideas.

Maya edits and streams from her dorm desk. Jordan codes across three windows and a game. Priya works and watches films in a one-bedroom.

03 · Positioning

Not a cheaper Vision Pro. The right-sized one.

“Air” already means the light, everyday one across Apple’s line-up.

For students and young creators who work, study and create in small spaces, Apple Vision Air is the spatial computer that gives you a studio’s worth of screens wherever you sit. Unlike headsets built for gaming, it’s light enough to wear for a whole study session and works with the Mac and iPhone you already own.

04 · Messaging pillars

One promise, three proofs.

The umbrella message promises space, not spectacle.

Light

Under 400 g. Wear it through a whole lecture review.

Limitless screens

A studio’s worth of screens on a dorm-room desk.

Made for making

Cut your next video on a screen the size of a wall.

05 · Go-to-market

From reveal to habit in seven months.

Developers first, creators second, then everyone at Back to School. Success means owners still wearing it every week at day 30.
