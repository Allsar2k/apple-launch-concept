# 03 · Positioning

## The product

**Apple Vision Air** is a concept for Apple's second spatial computer: a lighter, lower-priced sibling to Vision Pro. It keeps what makes Vision Pro different (eye-and-hand input, sharp text, the Mac Virtual Display) and drops the parts students don't need to start (the outward-facing EyeSight display, the premium materials). All specs below are targets for the concept, not real products.

| | Vision Pro | Vision Air (concept target) |
|---|---|---|
| Price | $3,499 | **$1,499**, or $124.92/month over 12 months with Apple Card Monthly Installments |
| Weight | Over a pound before the battery | **Under 400 g**, about 40% lighter |
| Built for | Professionals, early adopters | **Students and creators under 25** |
| Lead use | Immersive entertainment | **More screen in less space** |

## Positioning statement

> For **students and young creators who work, study and create in small spaces**, **Apple Vision Air** is the **spatial computer** that **gives you a studio's worth of screens wherever you sit**. Unlike **headsets built for gaming**, Vision Air is **light enough to wear for a whole study session and works with the Mac and iPhone you already own**.

## Why this position, and not the obvious one

- **Not "cheaper Vision Pro".** That frames the product as a compromise and makes the buyer feel like they settled. Vision Air is the right size for its owner, the way MacBook Air was for laptops.
- **Not "metaverse" or "the future".** The audience is tired of futurism they can't use today. The claim is a present-tense benefit: room.
- **Not gaming-first.** That is Meta's ground at a third of the price. Apple wins on making things and getting work done; games are a bonus, not the pitch.

## Reasons to believe

1. **Mac Virtual Display:** a MacBook becomes a huge, sharp screen. Students already own the Mac.
2. **Weight:** about 40% lighter than Vision Pro, the single most-cited comfort complaint addressed head on.
3. **Price and financing:** close to a MacBook Air, payable monthly alongside an iPhone.
4. **Made for making:** Final Cut Pro, spatial video capture on iPhone, and streaming tools on day one.

## Naming rationale

"Air" already means "the light, everyday one" across Apple's line-up (MacBook Air, iPad Air). Using it here tells buyers what to expect before they read a spec sheet, and frames Vision Pro as the step-up rather than the default.
